The Conversation

WHY MYTH
MATTERS NOW

We are living through a threshold moment. As AI accelerates — generating language, images, and ideas at unprecedented scale — a profound question surfaces: what is uniquely, irreversibly human about the way we create and connect?

"Myth isn't a relic of the past. It's the operating system of the human nervous system — and in a world flooded with artificial intelligence, it may be our most essential technology."

Brigitte and Emily bring together somatic leadership, mythic storytelling, and embodied practice to offer a different kind of intelligence — one rooted not in computation, but in the body's ancient wisdom. Their keynote at Human+ Tech Week explores how storytelling and embodiment are not just a counterpoint to AI, but its most powerful complement.
